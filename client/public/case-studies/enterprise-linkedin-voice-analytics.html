Enterprise LinkedIn Voice + Analytics

2025. Architecture for AI-assisted LinkedIn publishing.

Architecture

The problem

Teams needed AI-assisted publishing that preserved individual voice, approval controls, and performance visibility.

What I owned

I designed the platform architecture: voice controls, the approval path, and the analytics layers for a person, a team, and a sponsor.

What the design includes

  • Voice fingerprinting from writing samples, so pacing and structure stay with the author.
  • Role-based paths for admin, manager, user, and viewer roles.
  • Draft, review, approve, and schedule as the intended path. Auto-post is a policy choice, not a default.
  • Checks for repetition, overlapping language, and tone before a post can move forward.
  • Analytics views for engagement and growth, designed to sit beside the publishing workflow.

How a team would use it

The intended path is draft, review, approve, then schedule. This case does not document a team running that path in production.

Intended measures

These are the measures the design is built to track. They are not reported results.

  • Engagement rate per post and follower growth.
  • Posts per user per week and content approval rate.
  • Theme-level performance.
  • Pipeline and lead-quality attribution, if a CRM connection is turned on.

System detail

  • Context inputs: biography, brand profile, voice fingerprint, and prior performance.
  • Data inputs designed for sheets, CSV, and API ingestion.
  • Integration-ready design for LinkedIn, a model provider, Slack, HubSpot, and Salesforce.
  • Multi-tenant branding for a domain or a client-specific interface.
