Revenue Operations Classification

2025. Classification, scoring recommendations, and operating guidance.

Recommendations

The problem

CRM stages and pipeline data did not clearly show deal behavior or where managers should intervene. Revenue performance was inconsistent even where product-market signals were visible.

What I owned

I developed the classification, the scoring recommendations, and the operating guidance.

What changed

  • Win and loss behavior, segment conversion, and stage friction were mapped to probability tiers.
  • Account revenue, company size, and competitive context informed which signals belonged in the score.
  • CRM scoring recommendations prioritize higher-probability deals.
  • Operating guidance covers stalled opportunities and deals that cycle through the same stages.

How a team would use it

Managers use the score to decide where to intervene. Planning uses city and channel recommendations for budget and enablement. The published material describes that operating use. It does not document a production CRM rollout.

What we can say about the result

The case describes impact direction:

  • Clearer focus on segments that convert.
  • Forecast conversations that can use a score, not only a stage name.
  • Named points where a manager steps into a stalled deal.

A measured revenue lift is not part of this record.
