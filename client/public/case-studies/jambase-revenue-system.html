JamBase Revenue System

2026. Shipped operating system for JamBase sales and revenue operations.

Shipped system

The problem

LinkedIn, Apollo, and HubSpot were capable tools. Research, account context, outbound execution, CRM management, signals, and forecasting were fragmented across them.

What I owned

I owned the operating environment: how those tools connected, where AI classified signals, and where a person reviewed the result before it moved work forward.

What changed

I built one workspace for accounts, contacts, sequences, signals, and RevOps reporting. It uses live HubSpot sync and AI-assisted signal classification, with a reviewer in the loop.

How the team uses it

The team researches accounts, runs outreach, updates CRM records, and reviews pipeline and forecast activity in the same place. Marketing, communications prep, and customer context sit in that same motion.

What we can say about the result

The work contributed to more than $5 million in attributed sales and more than 200% year-over-year revenue growth on a new product. The system is one part of that sales motion. It did not produce those results by itself.

System detail

  • Frontend: React and Vite, TanStack Query, and a shared component system.
  • API: Express with Drizzle ORM over Postgres, and OpenAPI contracts checked with Zod.
  • Working areas: Accounts, Contacts, Sequences, Signals, News Signals, Parking Lot, and RevOps.
  • News Signals classifies RSS and pasted content. Reviewer overrides are kept for the next pass.
  • HubSpot sync covers target accounts and closed-won revenue. Apollo supports prospecting. LinkedIn supports account coverage and outbound.
