Genre Intelligence Engine

2025. Product and data proof for discovery and recommendations.

Proof of concept

The problem

Noisy, incomplete genre labels limited discovery and recommendation quality. The same artist or event could carry different labels depending on the source.

What I owned

I built the cleaning pipeline, the taxonomy and embedding outputs, and an inspectable personalized feed.

What changed

  • A first stage cleans and normalizes genre signals from artist and event metadata.
  • A second stage builds co-occurrence maps, taxonomy paths, embeddings, and clusters.
  • User preference and affinity outputs rank a personalized feed.
  • Taxonomy paths and cluster keys stay visible, so a grouping can be inspected.

How it is used

A product or data reviewer can see why an item was grouped or ranked. Synth, the live music product, uses this work in personalized discovery. The feed itself is a proof of concept.

What we can say about the result

The project produced taxonomies, embeddings, and an inspectable feed. That is product and data proof. It is not a revenue result, and it is not presented here as a finished production recommender.
